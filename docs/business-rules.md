# Business rules

Tickets have a title, description, type, priority, status, assignee, and
project.

- Type: Bug, Feature, or Improvement
- Priority: Low, Medium, or High
- Status: Open, In Progress, or Done
- Title: 3 to 100 characters
- Description: 10 to 1000 characters
- Assignee and project are required

All three statuses can change freely in either direction. Dashboard and ticket
summary counts include every ticket exactly once according to its current
status.
